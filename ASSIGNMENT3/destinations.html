Popular Destinations

1. Hunza Valley

Hunza Valley is one of the most beautiful tourist destinations in Pakistan. It is famous for its mountains, valleys, peaceful environment and culture.

2. Skardu

Skardu is located in Gilgit-Baltistan. It is famous for its beautiful mountains, lakes and natural scenery.

3. Swat Valley

Swat Valley is known for its green mountains, rivers, waterfalls and beautiful landscapes.

4. Naran Kaghan

Naran Kaghan is a popular tourist destination. Visitors can enjoy beautiful mountains, lakes, rivers and pleasant weather.

5. Lahore

Lahore is a historical city famous for its culture, food, architecture and historical buildings.

Activities for Tourists

  • Visit historical places
  • Explore mountains and valleys
  • Enjoy beautiful landscapes
  • Experience local culture
  • Try traditional Pakistani food
